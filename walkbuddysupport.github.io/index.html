Good to know.

How does WalkBuddy count my steps?

WalkBuddy uses steps from Apple Health on your iPhone, including steps recorded by your Apple Watch. Your usual walks count without starting a workout.

Do I spend coins to care for my buddy?

Care never uses coins. Walking unlocks care moments. Coins are for decorating your buddy’s home and choosing accessories in the Shop.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. You can walk with just your iPhone. If you have an Apple Watch, you can also check on your buddy and care for them from your wrist.

Which languages can we walk in?

WalkBuddy speaks English, Turkish, Japanese, German, Spanish and French.

Where can I get WalkBuddy?

WalkBuddy is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and Apple Watch. The download link will be right here when it’s ready.
