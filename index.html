WalkBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

WalkBuddy (“the app”) is a walking companion game for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what the app reads, what stays on your devices, what is sent to the services we use, why, for how long, and what rights you have.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Zeynep Gürkan (“we”). Contact: walkbuddysupport@gmail.com.

Steps and Apple Health

With your permission, WalkBuddy reads your step count and walking distance from Apple Health (including steps from Apple Watch). If Health is unavailable, it can use your iPhone’s Motion & Fitness step count instead. WalkBuddy never writes to Health and never reads any other health data.

Step counts, distance, your daily step goal, streaks, your buddy’s growth, treasures, coins and the names you enter stay on your own devices: your iPhone, its home-screen and lock-screen widgets, and, if you use it, your paired Apple Watch, which receives a summary of your buddy and today’s progress directly from your iPhone. None of this is uploaded to us or to any third party, sold, or used for advertising or marketing. There is no account and no cloud sync. When you share a picture of your buddy or your day from the app, iOS’s share sheet sends it only to the place you choose; we never receive a copy.

Legal basis

Checking whether your subscription is active is necessary to provide the service you bought (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)); we use RevenueCat, a service provider acting on our behalf, to do this. Usage events (Firebase Analytics) are sent only with your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)), except in the United States and Japan (when both your App Store country and your iPhone’s region are one of these), where usage events and crash reports are on unless you turn them off. The app asks once, on one privacy page at the start, sends nothing before you answer, and you can withdraw consent at any time in Settings › Steps & privacy › Share usage data; withdrawing does not affect events sent before. Ad results for Google Ads, Meta and TikTok are a third, separate consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)), asked on the same privacy page and off until you agree; neither SDK starts before that. In the United States and Japan (when both your App Store country and your iPhone’s region are one of these) we do not ask separately: the privacy page explains ad results, then iOS asks whether WalkBuddy may track, and that answer alone decides. If you do not allow tracking, nothing is sent to Google Ads, Meta or TikTok. Change it at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Withdraw it in Settings › Steps & privacy › Share ad results. Crash reporting is a separate, optional consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)), off by default outside the United States and Japan. Enable or withdraw it in Settings › Steps & privacy › Send crash reports. Firebase starts and fetches game settings through Remote Config only after either usage sharing or crash reporting is enabled. Remote Config supports game functionality on legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)); shipped defaults work without it. Pre-consent usage events are discarded, never uploaded retrospectively. Withdrawal stops future usage events; it does not erase data already uploaded. Random installation identifiers make these records pseudonymous, not fully anonymous. Data is collected automatically by the app through the services listed above. Sharing usage data, crash reports or ad results is never required: if you say no, every part of WalkBuddy works the same.

International transfers

Google, RevenueCat, Meta and TikTok may process this data in the United States and other countries. When data leaves the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, these providers protect it under their data processing terms, which use the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. See Firebase privacy information, RevenueCat’s privacy policy, Meta’s privacy policy and TikTok’s business products privacy policy.

How long data is kept

  • On your devices: until you use Settings › Start over or delete the app (and any device backup that contains it).
  • Firebase Analytics: event data tied to the app-instance identifier is kept for 2 months, then deleted automatically; aggregated reports do not identify an installation.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • Meta and TikTok: they keep ad results under their own retention rules, described in the policies linked above; we cannot change these.
  • RevenueCat: the anonymous subscription record is kept while it is needed to provide the subscription and to meet legal accounting obligations, then deleted.

Deleting your data

Settings › Steps & privacy › Start over erases everything WalkBuddy keeps on your iPhone and gives Firebase Analytics a new app-instance identifier for future events. It does not delete data already sent to Firebase or RevenueCat, and it does not change the Crashlytics, Firebase installation or RevenueCat identifiers. Data already sent is deleted when its retention period ends, or earlier if you ask us. Because we never collect your name or email, we find your records by the support code in Settings › Steps & privacy: tap “Ask us to delete sent data”, which emails us with that code, and we reply within 30 days. Ask before Start over, which gives your iPhone a new Firebase code. You can also turn off Health, Motion or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.

To ask for deletion or access, write to walkbuddysupport@gmail.com. Because these identifiers are not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, we may need your help to find your records (for example the date you installed the app or your App Store purchase receipt); if we cannot identify them, we will tell you. We answer within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, Turkey’s KVKK (Article 11) and similar laws, you may have the right to learn whether your data is processed, access it, have it corrected or deleted, receive it in a portable format, restrict how it is processed, object to processing based on legitimate interests, withdraw a consent at any time without affecting what was done before, learn who it is shared with, and claim compensation for unlawful processing. Write to walkbuddysupport@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey, the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

US state privacy rights (including California)

We do not sell personal data for money. If you turn on ad results, sending install, open and subscription events to Google, Meta and TikTok to measure our ads may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA), or as targeted advertising under similar state laws. Ad results are off until you agree: in the United States and Japan by allowing tracking in iOS’s prompt, elsewhere in the app’s privacy page. You can opt out at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking (United States and Japan) or in the app’s Settings › Steps & privacy › Share ad results (elsewhere).

The categories involved are identifiers (random app, installation and device identifiers, and the IDFA only if you allow tracking), internet or app activity (the usage events listed above), commercial information (subscription status and plan) and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information: your step and Health data never leave your devices. You may ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal data and to opt out of its sale or sharing, by writing to walkbuddysupport@gmail.com, directly or through an authorized agent. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

WalkBuddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
